### 👨‍💻 Professional Summary

Cross-functional technical professional specializing at the intersection of **Cybersecurity**, **Data Analytics**, and **Governance, Risk & Compliance (GRC)**. 

- 🛡️ **Cybersecurity & SOC**: Hands-on expertise in log correlation (SIEM, firewalls, IDS/IPS), threat monitoring (CrowdStrike EDR, Splunk), and vulnerability assessments (OWASP Top 10, MITRE ATT&CK, Burp Suite, Nmap).
- 📊 **Data Analytics & BI**: Proven experience architecting scalable ETL pipelines, exploratory data analysis (EDA), statistical modeling (Random Forest, ARIMA, LSTM), and interactive **Power BI / Kibana** KPI dashboards.
- 📋 **GRC & Audit Automation**: Experienced in conducting security gap assessments, internal audits, and policy enforcement aligned with **ISO/IEC 27001:2022**, **NIST CSF**, **GDPR**, and **SOC 2** frameworks.
- 🎓 **Education**: B.Tech in Computer Science (*Blockchain, IoT & Cyber Security*) — **Woxsen University** (2022 – 2026)
- 📜 **Research Paper**: Co-author, *AI-Based Fake OTP Detection: A Systematic Review and Proposed Detection Framework* (SciTech-25)
- ⚡ **Motto**: *"Failures are just restart points on the road to mastery."*
