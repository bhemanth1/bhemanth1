### 👨‍💻 Professional Summary

Cybersecurity engineer and analyst with hands-on experience in **security operations (SOC)**, **threat monitoring**, and **vulnerability assessment & penetration testing (VAPT)**. Proficient in investigating enterprise logs across SIEM, firewalls, and EDR systems, threat hunting against advanced attack vectors (SQLi, Phishing, Malware, Spoofing), and implementing rigorous risk mitigation frameworks (**ISO 27001**, **NIST**, **OWASP**, **MITRE ATT&CK**).

- 🎓 **Education**: B.Tech in Computer Science (Blockchain, IoT & Cyber Security) — *Woxsen University*
- 📍 **Location**: Hyderabad, India
- 💼 **Experience**: Penetration Tester at *Nexavise* | Data Engineer at *Opplane* | Security Intern at *Infotact* & *BlackBuck*
- 📜 **Key Research**: Co-author, *AI-Based Fake OTP Detection Framework* (SciTech-25)
- ⚡ **Motto**: *"Failures are just restart points on the road to mastery."*
