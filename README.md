### 👨‍💻 Professional Summary

Cybersecurity professional and analyst with hands-on experience in **security operations (SOC)**, **threat monitoring**, and **vulnerability assessment & penetration testing (VAPT)**. Skilled in analyzing security events across enterprise logs (SIEM, firewalls, IDS/IPS, EDR) and investigating threat activity including phishing, SQL injection, malware, and rogue reconnaissance. Experienced in building automated security pipelines and aligning architectures with industry standards (**OWASP Top 10**, **MITRE ATT&CK**, **NIST CSF**, **ISO 27001:2022**).

- 🎓 **Education**: B.Tech in Computer Science (*Blockchain, IoT & Cyber Security*) — **Woxsen University** (2022 – 2026)
- 📍 **Location**: Hyderabad, India
- 💼 **Industry Roles**: Penetration Tester at **Nexavise** | Data Engineer at **Opplane** | Cyber Security Intern at **Infotact Solutions** & **BlackBuck**
- 📜 **Research Paper**: Co-author, *AI-Based Fake OTP Detection: A Systematic Review and Proposed Detection Framework* (SciTech-25)
- ⚡ **Motto**: *"Failures are just restart points on the road to mastery."*
